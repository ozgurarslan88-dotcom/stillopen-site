I-285 · Atlanta, GA

Why Atlanta Forces Every Passing Truck Onto I-285

Road File · Published November 2026 · Facts checked against the sources below

The short version

In Atlanta, a semi with no business inside the city isn't allowed to drive through it. Georgia law keeps through trucks off I-75, I-85, I-20 and GA 400 inside the Perimeter, with fines up to $1,000. Every one of them is sent onto a single 64-mile ring road.

In 2013, that ring had more fatal crashes per mile than any other interstate in America, according to an analysis of NHTSA data.

Timeline

  • 2001 — Tanker fire on I-285.
  • 2013 — I-285 ranks first in fatal crashes per mile among U.S. interstates.
  • 2014 — A few inches of snow gridlock the city.
  • 2017 — Tanker spill on I-285.
  • 2022 — I-285 closure at Ashford Dunwoody.

Before you drive it

  • Driving a truck with no pickup, delivery or stop inside the Perimeter? Stay on I-285 and use truck-specific navigation.
  • In a car, plan your exit before you get on and move toward it early, one lane at a time.
  • Avoid the Top End at rush hour if you can.
  • Never cut in front of a truck to make an exit; the truck next to you may be a tanker.
  • Check Georgia 511 before you go. When the Perimeter closes, the alternatives fill up fast.
  • When snow or ice is forecast for Atlanta, believe it.
