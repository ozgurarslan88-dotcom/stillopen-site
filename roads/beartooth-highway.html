U.S. 212 · Beartooth Highway, MT/WY

Never Drive the Beartooth Highway on Opening Day

Road File · Published September 2026 · Facts checked against the sources below

The short version

The Beartooth Highway is a 68-mile stretch of U.S. Route 212 between Red Lodge, Montana and Cooke City, Montana, beside the Northeast Entrance of Yellowstone. Beartooth Pass sits at 10,947 feet in Wyoming, and most of the route runs above the treeline.

Plowing starts in mid-April, through drifts that have reached 26 feet. Opening day means less than people think: in 2022 the road opened in late May and closed three times before the end of June, the last time from flooding that washed it out south of Red Lodge.

Timeline

  • 1936 — The highway opens as an approach road to Yellowstone.
  • 2022 — Opens in late May, then closes three times before the end of June; floods wash it out south of Red Lodge.
  • September 2023 — Closed by about four inches of snow.
  • May 2024 — A winter storm delays the opening by a weekend.

Before you drive it

  • Check both the Montana and the Wyoming departments of transportation before you go. A clear report from one does not mean the whole road is open.
  • Check the morning you travel, not the night before. A few inches of snow at the top can close it overnight.
  • Do not treat the published opening date as a guarantee.
  • Carry extra clothing, food and water, as Montana's transportation department advises.
  • Know the fallback: Wyoming Highway 296.
